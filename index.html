Protein Pantry, independent storefront audit Open full PDF

Observed issue

The same quantity at two different prices

Three single packs selected through the quantity stepper produced ₹480, while the Pack of 3 option offered the same quantity for ₹420.

₹480Three single packs via the stepper
₹420Pack of 3, same quantity

The audit also documents a category navigation mismatch in how products are grouped and found.

Evidence

Read off the live storefront, 8 to 10 October 2026

Business implication

Resolve the pricing inconsistency before testing growth

Two prices for the same quantity give a shopper a reason to hesitate at the moment they are deciding. Correcting it would reduce the amount charged on affected orders by ₹60, so it is a correction rather than an uplift.

Any conversion benefit from removing that hesitation is a hypothesis and would need testing before anyone claimed it.

Proposed change

Make the pack choice explicit, and keep it

  • Make pack selection explicit rather than inferred from a quantity stepper.
  • Preserve the chosen pack and its price all the way through to the cart.
  • Make discovery match shopping intent through clearer product type and need based navigation.

Proposals, not implemented

Validation

How I would check whether it worked

  • Check pack selection and cart consistency directly.
  • Test across the products, pack sizes and devices specified in the audit.
  • For the proposed experiments, monitor revenue per product page visitor and pack mix.
  • Use conversion and gross margin per order as guardrails.

Independent academic case study. Protein Pantry did not commission this audit and is not a client or employer. The storefront may have changed since the dates above.
